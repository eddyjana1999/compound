Description from listing.md

Most compound interest calculators answer the wrong question. They show you the gross figure — the number before your fund takes its fee and before the tax authority takes its share. Compound shows you what is actually left. WHAT IT DOES Enter a starting amount, a monthly contribution, an expected annual return and a time horizon. Add your management fee and capital gains rate if you want them — they are optional, and off by default. You get a full breakdown: everything you paid in, everything the market earned, everything the fees took, everything the tax took, and the net figure at the end. The numbers reconcile exactly, so the breakdown always adds up to the headline. BUILT FOR ANYWHERE 49 currencies and 8 languages, with full right-to-left layout in Arabic and Hebrew. PRIVATE BY DEFAULT No account, no sign-in, no server. Your calculations never leave your phone. Compound is an estimation tool. It assumes a constant rate of return, which no real market provides, and it is not investment advice.

What is real and what is guessed

Real: the icon, all five screenshots, the name, subtitle and description — pulled straight from the project.

Guessed: the download size, the provider name, and the 9:41 status bar. Apple fills those in itself.

“No Ratings” is what everyone starts with. It stays that way until real users rate you, and it is the single biggest drag on early conversion. Worth asking your first few users directly.

The one thing to fix first

On a phone the store shows the icon, the name, the subtitle and roughly two screenshots before anyone scrolls. That is the whole pitch.

Right now the first screenshot is your history list — a screen that means nothing to someone who has never used the app. Consider leading with the results screen instead: it is the only one that shows the promise, that fees and tax come off the number.

Privacy Policy

Compound — investment calculator · Last updated 30 August 2026

The short version

  • Your calculations never leave your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing is sent to us, ever.
  • The only thing that collects anything is the advertising, run by Google AdMob.
  • Paying to remove the ads stops that collection entirely.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into Compound — starting amounts, contributions, rates, time horizons — and everything it saves for you, including your calculation history, chosen currency, language and theme, is stored only in your phone's own app storage.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. We have no server and no database, so there is no copy of your figures for us to read, lose, or be asked to hand over. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the ads collect

Compound is free and is paid for by advertising, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, and to measure whether they worked, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address, and the approximate location it implies;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • interactions with the ads themselves.

This is collected by Google, not by us — we never receive it and never see it. What Google does with it is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before any tracking. Decline and you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form appears the first time you open the app. You can reopen it any time from the app's Settings → Ad privacy to change or withdraw what you agreed to.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your phone's own settings, on either platform, which breaks the link to everything collected before.
  • You can remove the advertising entirely with the one-off in-app purchase. Once it is active the app makes no ad requests at all, so nothing further is collected.

Purchases

The option to remove ads is a single purchase handled entirely by Apple or Google. Your payment details go to them and never pass through the app. We receive no card number, no name and no billing address — only the confirmation that the purchase happened, which the app stores on your device so it remembers you have paid.

Children

Compound is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect anything from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing.

In practice, we hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to return or erase — the data in the app is already yours and already on your device. For the advertising data described above, the controller is Google, and those requests should be directed to Google. You can also exercise the choices listed under Your choices above at any time, without asking anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something it does not collect today, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change before that version ships.
